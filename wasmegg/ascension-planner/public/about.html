About and privacy

Last updated Oct 24, 2026. Short version: your save stays on your computer, nothing goes to the leaderboard unless you press a button, and your player ID is never sent to it.

What this is

An unofficial fan tool for planning virtue ascensions in Egg, Inc. It is built on Joo's Auto Ascension Planner and the wasmegg tools. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Auxbrain, the makers of Egg, Inc.

What stays on your computer

The planner runs in your browser. Your plans, settings, runs saved for later and saved answers are kept in your browser's own storage on your computer.

That includes your game save and your player ID. The latest save is kept so the page opens quickly, and saves that a saved run or answer needs are kept with it. Nothing expires them. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Where your save comes from

The planner fetches your save from the game's server through a small relay that Williamthe5thc runs on Cloudflare. The relay passes your player ID to the game server and your save back to your browser. It stores nothing, logs nothing, and only talks to the game's servers. Cloudflare, who host it, keep ordinary request statistics. The planner does not send your save or your player ID anywhere else.

What is sent, and when

Nothing goes to the leaderboard until you tick the box that says you understand what is sent and then press a button such as Share this result, Find and submit or Send it now. Once you have ticked it, this browser remembers that for that account (untick Remember my choice to have it for this visit only), and you can withdraw it any time: from then on nothing more is sent, not even from a run already going. One exception: Science › Submit a sweep sends a public row and its CSV when you press Submit this sweep, without that tick.

Two things are sent without a press. The route you follow can be saved online by itself: the first answer for Fastest to 490 TE and for Egg Day 2027, when you follow nothing for that goal (see The route you follow, saved online). And each open copy of the page sends its version, at most once a day, unless your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track (see Which version of the page you have open).

  • A leaderboard row. The route and its times, your start and end times and time zone, your starting TE and target, the search settings, your artifacts, stones and research that the plan uses, the days each ascension takes, how long the search ran and on what kind of computer (cores, workers, minutes), and the name you chose to be credited as, if any. If you set them, the row also has your awake hours (the times of day you said you are awake, shown in the Schedule column) and the days you marked as time off. It always has the weekday your plan starts on. Rows are public. A row also says where the plan started from: whether the planner used a save it had just fetched, the save kept in this browser, a save file, a saved Manual plan or an account switch; the Truth Eggs your save has claimed and the ones still pending for each egg, kept apart; your save's time and a short code made from that time and your eggs, never from your player ID; the eggs delivered for each egg at the start; whether the plan starts now, at a time you set, or at a time handed over from a search; and how long the page had been open and how old the planner's version was. If you tick "Share my instant answer with my runs" (on My runs), a row you send also has your instant answer for that run: the route (the stops), the finish date, the TE, and when it was worked out. It is shown on your public run so others can compare. Without that tick it is not sent.
  • The CSV of every route the search priced. Nothing is sent until you tick the agreement, which is off by default. Once you agree, "Send my CSV too" starts ticked, and you can untick it. The CSV is public too: anyone can download the CSV of a row. A sweep run from Science always sends one.
  • Private diagnostics (off unless you tick them): browser and system, cores, workers, memory use, and whether the tab crashed, to help find bugs. They are never shown publicly.
  • Search results beside the instant answer (off unless you tick "Share this result" or "Share my search results"): the route the instant answer gave, and the best result of a Fast, Exact or Custom search on your account for the same goal (it is in the record's exact field, which before Oct 23, 2026 held the price of the instant answer's own route), with the settings used, so we can see how close the instant answer is, with the same note as a row on where it started from. They are anonymous, sent only with that consent, and never shown publicly.
  • Progress sends (off unless you turn on "Send my progress every N min", or press "Send best so far"): the best route so far, as a row marked in progress, plus the CSV so far if that box is ticked. The final send replaces it.
  • The phone watch (off unless you turn it on): a status message from a long run, so your phone can show progress. It is encrypted in your browser before it leaves. The key is only in the link you open on your phone (after the #), so the server cannot read it. If you also turn on phone notifications, short notes go through ntfy.sh, and anyone who knows your notification topic can read them.

Sends are anonymous unless you type a name into the "Credit me as" box.

Help improve the planner, the box under the tabs, asks once in each browser. Every choice in it starts unticked. Its Yes turns on the choices you tick (sharing runs, the CSV, private diagnostics and "Share my search results") and your credit, for the account on screen only; anything you leave unticked stays off, and you can turn it on later in Setup. Another account is asked with a one-line strip. It adds nothing new to what is sent, and nothing goes until you press a button, except the checks you agreed to keep sharing. No thanks changes nothing and is not asked again. Change or withdraw it in Setup › Help improve the planner. Withdrawing turns off sharing for every account in this browser that said yes there; another account that agreed only under a share button keeps that choice. What was already sent stays.

This browser keeps your answer in a record called aap.study. It holds the answer (yes, no thanks or withdrawn), when you answered, the ticks, whether you chose a name or to stay anonymous, and a scrambled tag for each account that answered, made in this browser so it never shows the player ID. It holds no player ID and no name, and it is never sent.

The route you follow, saved online

When you press Follow this route (or Follow it, or Restore), the route you follow for that goal is saved online, under a code made from your player ID. That is so another device shows your route when you enter your ID there: loading your account reads the saved routes, and the newer of the saved one and the one on that device is the one followed. Anyone who knows your player ID can make the same code, so they can see the route you follow, and change it. If it couldn't be saved when you pressed it (you were offline, say), it is saved once the next time your account is loaded, unless a newer one is saved online by then. If it still isn't saved, My runs, Right now, says so under the route, with Save it online. The first answer for Fastest to 490 TE and for Egg Day 2027 becomes the route you follow and is saved online, unless you already follow one. That is the instant answer as its box shows it, or a Fast, Exact or Custom run that becomes your route on My runs, Right now, whichever comes first. It is saved once, the same way as a press, and only after your saved routes were read, so a route you follow on another device is never replaced. After that, a better answer only asks you (Follow it or Keep mine). Every other goal (another Highest TE by a date, a Fastest to N TE target of your own) is saved only when you press Follow this route. Nothing else saves it.

What is stored: the route (the stops), the goal, when you set it, where it came from (the instant answer, a Fast, Exact or Custom run, or picked by hand), when the plan starts and when it finishes, its TE and time to spare, the awake hours window you used, the time of your save, and the simulator and planner version. Never your player ID or a name. Your days, time off and no prestige days are not stored.

The last 5 versions for each goal are kept. To put an earlier one back, open Earlier versions under the route you follow on My runs, Right now, and press Restore: it is saved again as the newest. At most 30 changes an hour are saved. Using the planner's route again only changes this browser: the saved route stays online, and another device keeps following it.

What is never sent to the leaderboard

  • Your player ID. It is not a field in anything that is sent. The planner removes anything shaped like one from names, notes and the CSV before sending, and the server removes it from names and notes again.
  • Your game save.

Names and notes you type are public, so keep them free of anything personal. The time zone, start time and inventory in a row can also point to one account if someone knows the account well.

The random browser code

The first time this browser sends something, it makes a random code and keeps it in your browser. It is not made from your player ID. The server stores only a scrambled version of it (a hash). It is how you find your own rows, including anonymous ones, how a later run replaces your earlier "best so far" row, and how you can put a name on an anonymous row later. If you send under a name, rows named from the same code are shown as coming from the same account. Anonymous rows are not linked this way. Clearing your browser's site data makes a new code, and the old rows can no longer be claimed from this browser.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Sent data goes to a small service we run on Cloudflare (a Cloudflare Worker with Cloudflare's storage). The planner's pages are served separately.

What Kept for
Leaderboard rows and their CSVs No time limit. They stay until they are removed by hand. Hiding a row only hides it from public views.
Private diagnostics, instant-answer checks, refused results 180 days, then deleted.
The routes you follow No time limit. The last 5 versions of each goal, for up to 8 goals.
Version counts 30 days, then deleted.
Phone-watch messages 7 days after the last update, then deleted.
Your IP address Not stored in any record. It is used only to slow down floods of requests, held for 2 minutes inside a temporary key.

Cloudflare, as the host, may keep its own logs. We don't control or see those.

Hiding or removing your rows

Hide from the public is one click on your own rows (in the row details, or on the My runs tab, which lists hidden rows too). It hides them from every public view and stops other people downloading their CSV. You and the planner's maintainer can still see them, and Show again undoes it. A hidden row leaves the public lists within about a minute.

For anything else, message Williamthe5thc on Discord and say which rows are yours (your name on the Leaderboard, or the row's ID). The same person handles results that were refused: the message you get says "Contact Williamthe5thc on Discord with this code".

Rows and CSVs are public while they are showing, so someone may have saved a copy before you hid them.

Cookies, ads and counters

  • No cookies. No ads. No accounts.
  • Two page-view counters run on the main planner page. GoatCounter (the counter of the upstream wasmegg tools, run by Carpet) is a simple visit counter that says it does not use cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookie-free too: it gives this site's owner aggregated page views. Both see the usual details a browser sends, such as your IP address and browser. GoatCounter is sent the page's path only, without the part after the ?. If you open a link with your player ID in its address, the page takes the ID out of the address as soon as it has read it, but don't share links like that.
  • Pages also load pictures and event data from other sites (artifact and egg images, and the event list from GitHub). The cat picture in the plan summary loads from another site only after you click for it.

Which version of the page you have open

Each open copy of the planner tells our server which version it is, at most once a day: just the version, nothing else. We keep only a count per version per day, for 30 days, so we know when old versions are still in use. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, nothing is sent.

Security

The site sends standard security headers and a strict script policy. To report a problem, see /.well-known/security.txt (it points to GitHub issues).
