About and privacy

Last updated 21 Oct 2026. Short version: your save stays on your computer, nothing goes to the leaderboard unless you press a button, and your player ID is never sent to it.

What this is

An unofficial fan tool for planning virtue ascensions in Egg, Inc. It is built on Joo's Auto Ascension Planner and the wasmegg tools. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Auxbrain, the makers of Egg, Inc.

What stays on your computer

The planner runs in your browser. Your plans, settings, saved runs and saved answers are kept in your browser's own storage on your computer.

That includes your game save and your player ID. The latest save is kept so the page opens quickly, and saves that a saved run or answer needs are kept with it. Nothing expires them. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Where your save comes from

The planner fetches your save from the game's server through a small relay that Williamthe5thc runs on Cloudflare. The relay passes your player ID to the game server and your save back to your browser. It stores nothing, logs nothing, and only talks to the game's servers. Cloudflare, who host it, keep ordinary request statistics. The planner does not send your save or your player ID anywhere else.

What is sent, and only when you press a button

Nothing goes to the leaderboard until you tick the box that says you understand what is sent and then press a button such as Share this result, Find and submit or Send it now. Once you have ticked it, this browser remembers that for that account (untick Remember my choice to have it for this visit only), and you can withdraw it any time: from then on nothing more is sent, not even from a run already going.

  • A leaderboard row. The route and its times, your start and end times and time zone, your starting TE and target, the search settings, your artifacts, stones and research that the plan uses, the days each leg takes, how long the search ran and on what kind of computer (cores, workers, minutes), and the name you chose to be credited as, if any. Rows are public.
  • The CSV of every route the search priced. Nothing is sent until you tick the agreement, which is off by default. Once you agree, "Send my CSV too" starts ticked, and you can untick it. The CSV is public too: anyone can download the CSV of a row. The Science sweep page always sends one.
  • Private diagnostics (off unless you tick them): browser and system, cores, workers, memory use, and whether the tab crashed, to help find bugs. They are never shown publicly.
  • Instant-answer checks (off unless you turn on "share this check" or "keep sharing my checks"): the route the instant answer gave, what Check exactly found for it, and the settings used, so we can see how close the instant answer is. They are never shown publicly.
  • Progress sends (off unless you turn on "Send my progress every N min", or press "Send best so far"): the best route so far, as a row marked in progress, plus the CSV so far if that box is ticked. The final send replaces it.
  • The phone watch (off unless you turn it on): a status message from a long run, so your phone can show progress. It is encrypted in your browser before it leaves. The key is only in the link you open on your phone (after the #), so the server cannot read it. If you also turn on phone notifications, short notes go through ntfy.sh, and anyone who knows your notification topic can read them.

Sends are anonymous unless you type a name into the "Credit me as" box.

Help improve the planner, the box under the tabs, asks once in each browser. Its Yes turns on the same choices you leave ticked (sharing runs, the CSV, private diagnostics and "Keep sharing my checks") and your credit, for the account on screen only; anything you untick stays undecided, as it was. Another account is asked with a one-line strip. It adds nothing new to what is sent, and nothing goes until you press a button, except the checks you agreed to keep sharing. No thanks changes nothing and is not asked again. Change or withdraw it in Setup › Help improve the planner; withdrawing stops what is sent from then on, and what was already sent stays.

What is never sent to the leaderboard

  • Your player ID. It is not a field in anything that is sent. The planner removes anything shaped like one from names, notes and the CSV before sending, and the server removes it from names and notes again.
  • Your game save.

Names and notes you type are public, so keep them free of anything personal. The time zone, start time and inventory in a row can also point to one account if someone knows the account well.

The random browser code

The first time this browser sends something, it makes a random code and keeps it in your browser. It is not made from your player ID. The server stores only a scrambled version of it (a hash). It is how you find your own rows, including anonymous ones, how a later run replaces your earlier "best so far" row, and how you can put a name on an anonymous row later. If you send under a name, rows named from the same code are shown as coming from the same account. Anonymous rows are not linked this way. Clearing your browser's site data makes a new code, and the old rows can no longer be claimed from this browser.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Sent data goes to a small service we run on Cloudflare (a Cloudflare Worker with Cloudflare's storage). The planner's pages are served separately.

What Kept for
Leaderboard rows and their CSVs No time limit. They stay until they are removed by hand. Hiding a row only hides it from public views.
Private diagnostics, instant-answer checks, refused results 180 days, then deleted.
Phone-watch messages 7 days after the last update, then deleted.
Your IP address Not stored in any record. It is used only to slow down floods of requests, held for 2 minutes inside a temporary key.

Cloudflare, as the host, may keep its own logs. We don't control or see those.

Hiding or removing your rows

Hide from the public is one click on your own rows (in the row details, or on the My runs tab, which lists hidden rows too). It hides them from every public view and stops other people downloading their CSV. You and the planner's maintainer can still see them, and Show again undoes it. A hidden row leaves the public lists within about a minute.

For anything else, message Williamthe5thc on Discord and say which rows are yours (your name on the board, or the row's ID). The same person handles results that were refused: the message you get says "Contact Williamthe5thc on Discord with this code".

Rows and CSVs are public while they are showing, so someone may have saved a copy before you hid them.

Cookies, ads and counters

  • No cookies. No ads. No accounts.
  • Two page-view counters run on the main planner page. GoatCounter (the counter of the upstream wasmegg tools, run by Carpet) is a simple visit counter that says it does not use cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookie-free too: it gives this site's owner aggregated page views. Both see the page address you open and the usual details a browser sends, such as your IP address and browser. If you open a link that has your player ID in its address, they would see that address, so don't share links like that.
  • Pages also load pictures and event data from other sites (artifact and egg images, and the event list from GitHub). The cat picture in the plan summary loads from another site only after you click for it.

Security

The site sends standard security headers and a strict script policy. To report a problem, see /.well-known/security.txt (it points to GitHub issues).
